# V162 · PLATFORM SCALE — Sangeet Paul Choudary
Tier 3 · Partnerships · Tree Memory

## ROOT
Platform businesses (marketplaces, app stores, social networks) operate under fundamentally different rules than traditional pipeline businesses. Choudary's framework: platforms create value by enabling interactions between producers and consumers — they don't produce anything themselves. The platform's core job: (1) Enable the core interaction (the value exchange between producer and consumer). (2) Reduce friction at each step of that interaction. (3) Solve the chicken-and-egg problem (which side to attract first?). (4) Design the curation and reputation systems that maintain quality. (5) Monetize without destroying the value-creating interactions.

## TRUNK
Platform design principles: (1) Core interaction: what does the producer contribute and what does the consumer receive? The platform's job is to make this interaction happen smoothly and repeatedly. (2) Chicken-and-egg: solve by subsidizing one side (Uber subsidized drivers first), by solving a standalone problem for one side first (OpenTable gave restaurants reservation management before diner apps), or by providing the marketplace with pre-seeded supply. (3) Friction reduction: at the interaction level — discovery (matching), transaction (payment, trust), fulfillment (delivery, quality assurance). (4) Network effects: same-side (more drivers = lower wait times for ALL drivers? No — more drivers = more competition. Negative same-side effect.) Cross-side (more drivers = better experience for riders = more riders = more demand for drivers. Positive cross-side effect.) (5) Curation v Open: platforms must choose between open (anyone can participate) and curated (quality screening). This is the most important strategic choice.

## FRUIT
- WHEN building a platform → APPLY core interaction definition: what is the single most important interaction between producer and consumer?
- WHEN growth is stalled → APPLY network effect diagnosis: is the problem on the supply side, demand side, or matching?

## SEEDS
- "Platforms don't produce value — they enable it. Your job is to make the core interaction happen."
- "Solve the chicken-and-egg problem by seeding one side first."
- "The most important platform choice: open vs curated."

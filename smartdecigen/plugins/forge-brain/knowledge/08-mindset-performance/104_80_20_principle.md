# V104 · THE 80/20 PRINCIPLE — Richard Koch
Tier 3 · Mindset · Tree Memory

## ROOT
Pareto's original observation (80% of Italian land owned by 20% of people) is not just an interesting fact — it's a universal law of productivity. Koch expands it: 80% of results come from 20% of efforts. 80% of revenue comes from 20% of customers. 80% of value comes from 20% of features. The practical implication: find your 20% and double down on it. Kill or delegate the 80% that produces little.

## TRUNK
Key applications: (1) Time: 20% of your work produces 80% of your results. Identify that 20% and protect it ruthlessly. (2) Customers: 20% of customers generate 80% of profits. Fire the bottom 20% that consume resources and produce nothing. (3) Products: 20% of SKUs generate 80% of sales. Kill the long tail. (4) People: 20% of team members produce 80% of output. Pay them disproportionately. (5) Happiness: 20% of activities produce 80% of joy. Do more of those. The principle compounds: 80/20 applied to the 20% (top 4% producing 64% of results). Time Revolution: work 2 days a week at 4x productivity = 8 normal days of output.

## FRUIT
- WHEN spread too thin → APPLY 80/20 audit: what 20% of activities/customers/products produce 80% of results?
- WHEN prioritizing → APPLY the double 80/20: identify the 4% that produces 64% of value.
- WHEN a customer is draining resources → APPLY the bottom-20 fire: cut the 20% of customers who consume 80% of support.

## SEEDS
- "80% of results from 20% of effort. Find the 20%. Double down."
- "The bottom 20% of customers consume 80% of your support. Fire them."
- "Double 80/20: 4% of activities produce 64% of results."
- "Achieve more by doing less of the right things."

## GRAFTS
- → Essentialism: the disciplined pursuit of the vital 20%.
- → The ONE Thing: the single most impactful activity in the 20%.

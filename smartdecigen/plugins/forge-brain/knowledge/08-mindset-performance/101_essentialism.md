# V101 · ESSENTIALISM — Greg McKeown
Tier 3 · Mindset · Tree Memory

## ROOT
Essentialism is not about getting more done — it's about getting the RIGHT things done. It's the disciplined pursuit of less but better. The core practice: constantly ask "is this the most important thing I should be doing with my time and resources right now?" and have the courage to say no to everything else. Essentialism is a mindset, not a time-management technique.

## TRUNK
Three steps: (1) Explore — give yourself space to discern what is essential. Sleep, play, and solitude are not luxuries; they are inputs to better judgment. (2) Eliminate — say no gracefully and firmly. "If it isn't a clear yes, it's a clear no." The 90% rule: rate every option 0-100, keep only 90+. (3) Execute — build routines that make execution effortless. Buffer time (50% more than you think). Remove obstacles. Celebrate small wins. The Essentialist's mantra: "Less but better."

## FRUIT
- WHEN overwhelmed → APPLY the 90% rule: rate every commitment. Anything below 90%, decline.
- WHEN calendar is someone else's → APPLY the "if it isn't a clear yes" rule. Protect maker time.
- WHEN defaulting to "yes" → APPLY the pause: "let me check my calendar and get back to you." Buy time to think.

# V60 · THINK AND GROW RICH — Napoleon Hill
Tier 6 · Mindset · Tree Memory

## ROOT — the deepest truth
Whatever the mind can conceive and BELIEVE, it can achieve — thoughts are THINGS, and by controlling your thoughts through DEFINITE PURPOSE, FAITH, and PERSISTENCE, you can transmute desire into its physical equivalent, regardless of starting circumstances.

## TRUNK — core thesis
Hill spent 20 years interviewing the wealthiest men of his era (Carnegie, Ford, Rockefeller, Edison, Morgan) and distilled their success into 13 PRINCIPLES. The foundation: BURNING DESIRE — not a wish but an obsessive, white-hot purpose that consumes you. AUTOSUGGESTION: program your subconscious through repeated affirmation of your desire. SPECIALIZED KNOWLEDGE: general knowledge is useless; you need specialized knowledge organized and applied. IMAGINATION: synthetic (recombining existing ideas) and creative (the infinite intelligence channeled through intuition). ORGANIZED PLANNING: desire must be translated into a concrete plan with a MASTERMIND ALLIANCE — a group of like-minded people who support and challenge each other. DECISION: the habit of making decisions quickly and changing them slowly (the opposite of most people). PERSISTENCE: sustained effort sustained by desire, knowledge, and planning. And the most controversial but central: FAITH — visualized, emotionalized belief in the attainment of your goal.

## BRANCHES & LEAVES

### Branch 1 — Burning desire: the starting point
├─ Not a wish, not a hope — a BURNING DESIRE that makes failure impossible because you simply won't stop
├─ Hill's six-step method: fix in your mind the EXACT amount of money you want, what you'll give in return, the exact date, the definite plan, read the statement aloud twice daily, SEE yourself already in possession
├─ The difference between the wealthy and the poor: desire intensity — most people want success like they want a vacation, not like a drowning person wants air
└─ LEAF: when a founder is inconsistent or easily discouraged, test their desire intensity: is this a "would be nice" or a "must have at any cost"? The intensity of the desire determines the persistence it fuels

### Branch 2 — Faith: visualized belief
├─ Faith is not religious — it's a state of mind achievable through auto-suggestion: repetitive affirmation + visualization
├─ "Faith is the head chemist of the mind" — it transforms the ordinary into the extraordinary by activating the subconscious
├─ Practical method: repeat your desire statement + see yourself already having achieved it — the subconscious cannot distinguish between a vividly imagined experience and a real one
└─ LEAF: when a founder's confidence wavers, prescribe the visualization ritual: 10 minutes daily, close your eyes, SEE the successful outcome with all senses (what does it look like, feel like, sound like?) — this reprograms the mind toward belief

### Branch 3 — The Mastermind: coordinated intelligence
├─ "Two minds working together create a third, invisible, intangible force" — the mastermind principle
├─ A mastermind alliance: two or more people coordinating in a spirit of HARMONY toward a DEFINITE purpose
├─ Each person brings specialized knowledge, perspective, and energy; the group amplifies each individual
├─ Hill's most striking claim: the mastermind is the key to nearly ALL great fortunes
└─ LEAF: when a founder is going it alone (no co-founder, no advisory board, no peer group), prescribe a mastermind: a meeting of equals committed to each other's success, meeting regularly with a shared purpose — not a networking group but an accountability council

### Branch 4 — Decision and persistence
├─ "The inability to make a decision is one of the leading causes of failure"
├─ Successful people make decisions quickly (after gathering facts) and change them SLOWLY; unsuccessful people decide slowly and change quickly
├─ Persistence is not a virtue — it's a HABIT built on: definiteness of purpose, desire, self-reliance, definite plans, accurate knowledge, cooperation, willpower, and habit itself
└─ LEAF: when a founder vacillates (changing direction monthly, never committing), apply Hill's rule: make this decision TODAY; write it down; you will not revisit it for 90 days. The enforced commitment is the discipline that builds decision fitness

### Branch 5 — Specialized knowledge and imagination
├─ General knowledge is not power — it's POTENTIAL. Organized, specialized knowledge applied to a definite goal is power
├─ Two forms of imagination: synthetic (rearranging existing ideas into new combinations — most business innovation) and creative (tapping into the "infinite intelligence" — intuition, hunches, inspiration)
├─ The richest people are not the most educated — they are the best at organizing specialized knowledge THEY don't personally possess (they hire experts)
└─ LEAF: when a founder feels inadequate because they lack formal education or expertise, reframe: you don't need to KNOW everything — you need to ORGANIZE the knowledge through the best minds you can gather; the mastermind provides the knowledge the individual lacks

### Branch 6 — Overcoming fear: the six basic fears
├─ Hill identified six basic fears: POVERTY (most destructive), CRITICISM (loss of approval — paralyzes initiative), ILL HEALTH (hypochondria), LOSS OF LOVE OF SOMEONE, OLD AGE (fear of irrelevance), DEATH
├─ All fears are the result of bad mental programming — they can be replaced by DESIRE
├─ The fear of poverty is the most dangerous for entrepreneurs: it creates indecision, doubt, and worry that blocks the mind from the resources it needs
└─ LEAF: when a founder is indecisive or anxious, diagnose which of the six fears is active — usually fear of poverty or criticism; name it, separate it from reality, and counter-program with desire and faith

## FRUIT — how SmartDecigen applies this
- WHEN a founder lacks persistence → APPLY desire intensity check: is this a burning desire or a mild wish? Strengthen the desire before expecting persistence.
- WHEN a founder is isolated → APPLY mastermind formation: identify 2-4 peers/founders for a regular accountability and support group.
- WHEN a founder vacillates on decisions → APPLY fast-slow rule: decide now, commit for 90 days, revisit then.
- WHEN a founder suffers from imposter syndrome (fears criticism/inadequacy) → APPLY specialized knowledge reframe: you don't need to know everything — you need to organize great minds who do.
- WHEN a founder is consumed by worry → APPLY fear diagnosis: which of the six fears is active? Replace it with a burning desire for a specific outcome.

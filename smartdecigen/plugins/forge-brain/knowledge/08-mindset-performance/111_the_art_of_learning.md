# V111 · THE ART OF LEARNING — Josh Waitzkin
Tier 3 · Mindset · Tree Memory

## ROOT
Waitzkin — chess prodigy (subject of Searching for Bobby Fischer) turned Tai Chi world champion — distills a lifetime of mastery into principles. The core insight: mastery is not about accumulating techniques; it's about developing a deep, intuitive relationship with the fundamentals. The progression: (1) learn the principles, (2) internalize them through deep, focused practice until they become unconscious, (3) develop the ability to access peak performance states on demand, (4) turn adversity into growth. "Growth comes at the point of resistance."

## TRUNK
Key principles: (1) Investment in loss — losing is the fastest path to improvement if you study the loss. Give yourself permission to be bad at something to get good at it. (2) Making smaller circles — mastery is depth, not breadth. Internalize fundamental techniques until they are microscopic and instantaneous. (3) The downward spiral — high performers recognize when they're slipping into a negative state and have a trigger to reset. (4) Beginner's mind — approach every situation with curiosity, even when you're an expert. (5) Building the trigger — condition a peak performance state to a simple physical trigger (deep breath, specific music, visualization) so you can access it on demand. (6) Stress and recovery — interval training for the mind: intense focus → deliberate recovery → intense focus. The ability to recover quickly is a trainable skill.

## FRUIT
- WHEN facing a setback → APPLY investment in loss: what is this loss teaching? Study it without ego.
- WHEN performance is inconsistent → APPLY trigger conditioning: build a 30-second routine to access peak state on demand.
- WHEN skills feel shallow → APPLY making smaller circles: go deeper on fundamentals, not broader on techniques.

## SEEDS
- "Growth comes at the point of resistance. Seek the discomfort."
- "Investment in loss: every defeat is data. Study it without ego."
- "Making smaller circles: depth over breadth. Internalize until microscopic."
- "Build a trigger. Access your peak state on demand."
- "Stress + recovery = growth. Train both."

## GRAFTS
- → Peak: the deliberate practice that makes Waitzkin's principles operational.
- → Flow: the optimal performance state he learned to access on demand.
- → Grit: the perseverance through loss that builds mastery.

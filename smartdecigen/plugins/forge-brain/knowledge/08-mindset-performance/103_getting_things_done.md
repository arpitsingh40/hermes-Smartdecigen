# V103 · GETTING THINGS DONE — David Allen
Tier 3 · Mindset · Tree Memory

## ROOT
Allen's core insight: your mind is for having ideas, not holding them. Stress and distraction come from open loops — commitments you've made but haven't captured in a trusted system. GTD provides a complete workflow: capture everything that has your attention → clarify what each item means and what to do → organize into lists → reflect regularly → engage with confidence. The result: mind like water — calm, clear, ready for anything.

## TRUNK
Five steps: (1) Capture — get everything out of your head. Every task, idea, commitment. Into an inbox. (2) Clarify — for each item: is it actionable? If no: trash, someday/maybe, or reference. If yes: what's the next action? Will it take <2 minutes? Do it now. (3) Organize — next actions list, projects list, waiting-for list, calendar, someday/maybe. (4) Reflect — weekly review. Process inboxes, update lists, get current. This is the keystone habit. (5) Engage — use context (where you are), time available, energy available, and priority to choose what to do.

## FRUIT
- WHEN overwhelmed by tasks → APPLY capture: write down every open loop. Externalize everything.
- WHEN inbox is overflowing → APPLY the 2-minute rule: if it takes <2 minutes, do it now. Don't defer.
- WHEN feeling scattered → APPLY weekly review: process all inboxes, update all lists. One hour every week.

## SEEDS
- "Your mind is for having ideas, not holding them. Externalize everything."
- "The 2-minute rule: if it takes less than 2 minutes, do it now."
- "Weekly review is the keystone habit. Without it, GTD collapses."
- "Next action: the specific, physical next step. Not 'plan event' — 'call venue for pricing.'"

# V70 · EXECUTION — Bossidy & Charan
Tier 4 · Founder Judgment · Tree Memory

## ROOT — the deepest truth
Strategy fails not because it's wrong but because it's never truly executed — and execution is not tactics, it's a discipline and a system that must be built into the organization's core processes, starting with who leads them and how they are held accountable.

## TRUNK — core thesis
Bossidy and Charan argue that the gap between a strategy and its results is always an execution gap, and closing it requires three core processes wired together: PEOPLE (getting the right people into the right jobs), STRATEGY (building a realistic plan that connects to the operating reality), and OPERATIONS (translating strategy into concrete near-term actions with assigned owners and deadlines). The leader's job is not to delegate execution — the leader IS the chief execution officer, personally driving the link between people, strategy, and operations through rigorous dialogue, follow-through, and accountability. The root cause of most execution failures: soft, vague language that lets people off the hook — execution demands specificity, dates, names, and consequences.

## BRANCHES & LEAVES

### Branch 1 — The leader's role in execution
├─ Execution is not a detail the CEO can delegate — it IS the CEO's primary job
├─ The leader must be deeply engaged in the operating details, not hovering above them
├─ Intellectual leadership without behavioral follow-through produces cynicism, not results
└─ LEAF: at every strategy review, demand a named owner and a specific date for each action item — if there is neither, the item is not real

### Branch 2 — The people process: getting the right person in the right job
├─ Most organizations evaluate people through vague categories ("good performer") rather than specific task-to-person fit
├─ The leader must personally know the top talent at least two layers down
├─ The people process evaluates against three criteria: does this person deliver results, does they grow people, does they understand the business deeply?
└─ LEAF: before any major initiative, review the leader assigned to it against the specific demands of THAT initiative — general competence is not enough

### Branch 3 — The strategy process: reality-based planning
├─ Most strategic plans are wishful thinking disconnected from operational capacity
├─ A strategy must answer: what is the external reality (competitive, market, regulatory)? What are our strengths and weaknesses? What is the specific path to results?
├─ The strategy document should be short enough that the person executing it can hold it in their head
└─ LEAF: test any strategic plan by asking "what would have to be true for this plan to fail?" — if the answer is vague, the plan is fantasy

### Branch 4 — The operations process: building the bridge from strategy to action
├─ An operating plan is not a budget — it's a set of prioritized, time-bound, ownership-assigned actions
├─ Each quarter must have specific milestones that, if hit, mean the strategy is on track
├─ The operations process includes a built-in review cadence: what was accomplished? What deviated? What changes are needed?
└─ LEAF: an operating plan without a weekly or biweekly review cadence is a wish list, not a plan

### Branch 5 — Robust dialogue (the foundation of all three processes)
├─ Execution requires honest, specific, often uncomfortable conversations — not polite presentations
├─ The hallmarks: data-driven, direct, open to disagreement, no hidden agendas
├─ The leader sets the tone by asking the hard questions first and welcoming being challenged
└─ LEAF: when a review presentation is smooth and without disagreement, suspect it — execution gaps hide in the silence

## FRUIT — how SmartDecigen applies this
- WHEN a strategy is presented → APPLY the execution test: name the owner, the date, and the consequence for each action — if absent, the strategy is not real.
- WHEN a hire is considered → APPLY the three-criteria screen: results × grows people × understands business.
- WHEN an operating plan is drafted → APPLY the reality check: what would have to fail for this plan to miss? Build the tripwire.
- WHEN a review is too smooth → APPLY the robust dialogue prompt: "what would you say if there were no consequences for telling the truth?"
- WHEN the founder delegates execution → APPLY the chief-execution-officer frame: these three processes cannot be delegated — only attended.

# V106 · REINVENTING ORGANIZATIONS — Frederic Laloux
Tier 3 · Leadership · Tree Memory

## ROOT
A new organizational paradigm is emerging. Laloux identified 12 organizations (including Buurtzorg, Patagonia, Morning Star) that operate on fundamentally different principles: self-management (no hierarchy, peer-based decisions), wholeness (bring your whole self to work), and evolutionary purpose (the organization has its own direction, not just the CEO's plan). These "Teal" organizations consistently outperform traditional hierarchies on engagement, innovation, and results.

## TRUNK
Three breakthroughs: (1) Self-management: replace hierarchy with peer-based processes. Decision-making via advice process (anyone can make any decision after seeking advice from affected parties and subject matter experts). No bosses, no titles. (2) Wholeness: create practices that invite people to bring their full humanity — emotions, intuition, spirituality — not just their "professional" mask. (3) Evolutionary purpose: the organization is seen as a living entity with its own sense of direction. Strategy emerges from listening, not from top-down planning. Buurtzorg (14,000 nurses, 50 back-office staff, highest patient satisfaction in Netherlands) is the exemplar.

## FRUIT
- WHEN hierarchy is slowing decisions → APPLY advice process: anyone can decide after consulting affected parties + experts.
- WHEN culture feels inauthentic → APPLY wholeness practices: start meetings with a personal check-in.
- WHEN strategy feels forced → APPLY evolutionary purpose: ask "what does the organization want to become?" not "what do I want it to become?"

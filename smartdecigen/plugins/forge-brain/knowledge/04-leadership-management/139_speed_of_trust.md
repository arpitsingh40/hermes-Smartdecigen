# V139 · THE SPEED OF TRUST — Stephen M.R. Covey
Tier 3 · Leadership · Tree Memory

## ROOT
Trust is not a soft, social virtue — it is a hard, measurable economic driver. Covey's thesis: trust always affects two measurable outcomes — speed and cost. When trust goes down, speed goes down and cost goes up (trust tax). When trust goes up, speed goes up and cost goes down (trust dividend). In business, trust is THE most significant predictor of long-term success across every relationship: customers, employees, partners, investors.

## TRUNK
The 5 Waves of Trust: (1) Self-Trust: credibility — the foundation. Integrity (do you do what you say?), Intent (what's your agenda?), Capabilities (are you relevant and capable?), Results (what's your track record?). (2) Relationship Trust: consistent behavior. The 13 Behaviors of high-trust leaders: talk straight, demonstrate respect, create transparency, right wrongs, show loyalty, deliver results, get better, confront reality, clarify expectations, practice accountability, listen first, keep commitments, extend trust. (3) Organizational Trust: alignment — systems, structures, and processes that build or destroy trust. (4) Market Trust: reputation — your brand. (5) Societal Trust: contribution — your company's impact on the world. Each wave builds on the one before.

## BRANCHES
- Trust tax vs dividend: after a crisis, every decision takes 3x longer and costs 2x more because trust is gone. Before the crisis, speed was high and cost was low. Measure trust by measuring speed and cost.
- The 13 Behaviors: the most powerful is "extend trust" — trusting others first creates a reciprocal obligation. The most violated is "keep commitments" — every broken promise, even small, taxes trust.
- Restoring trust: (1) acknowledge the violation immediately, (2) own it without defensiveness, (3) declare the specific behavior change, (4) demonstrate consistently over time. Trust is restored by behavior, not words.
- Organizational trust killers: secrecy, inconsistency, favoritism, broken promises, and blaming culture. Organizational trust builders: transparency, consistency, fairness, accountability, and learning culture.

## FRUIT
- WHEN a partnership or relationship has broken down → APPLY trust tax diagnosis: how much slower and more expensive is everything because trust is gone?
- WHEN leadership credibility is questioned → APPLY the 4 Cores: integrity, intent, capabilities, results. Which core is weakest?
- WHEN building culture → APPLY the 13 Behaviors: audit which behaviors are strong and which are missing.
- WHEN a crisis hits → APPLY trust restoration: acknowledge immediately, own it, declare the change, demonstrate consistently.

## SEEDS
- "Trust is not soft. It's the single biggest driver of business speed and cost."
- "When trust is high, speed goes up and cost goes down. When trust is low, the reverse."

- "Extend trust first. It creates a reciprocal obligation."

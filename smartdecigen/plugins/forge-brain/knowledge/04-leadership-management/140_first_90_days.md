# V140 · THE FIRST 90 DAYS — Michael Watkins
Tier 3 · Leadership · Tree Memory

## ROOT
Transitions are periods of acute vulnerability — for the individual and the organization. Watkins' research on senior executive transitions shows that failures in the first 90 days almost never result from lack of competence but from failure to diagnose the situation, secure early wins, and build the right coalitions. The first 90 days is a predictable, learnable process.

## TRUNK
10 key principles: (1) Promote yourself — mentally prepare. (2) Accelerate your learning. (3) Match strategy to situation (STARS model: Startup, Turnaround, Accelerated growth, Realignment, Sustaining success). (4) Secure early wins (by end of month 2). (5) Negotiate success with your boss. (6) Achieve alignment (strategy, structure, systems, skills, culture). (7) Build your team. (8) Create coalitions. (9) Keep your balance. (10) Expedite everyone. The most common failure: applying the wrong STARS strategy. A turnaround leader crushes a startup. A startup leader fails in a realignment.

## FRUIT
- WHEN onboarding a new exec → APPLY STARS: what's the situation (startup/turnaround/acceleration/realignment/sustaining)? Match the 90-day plan to it.
- WHEN a new hire is struggling → APPLY early wins check: have they secured a visible, measurable win by day 60?

## SEEDS
- "The first 90 days is the most vulnerable period in any career. It's also the most learnable."
- "Match your strategy to the situation. A turnaround approach kills a startup."
- "Secure a visible, measurable win by day 60. Momentum is everything."

## GRAFTS
- → Who: the hiring process BEFORE the first 90 days.
- → High Output Management: the ongoing management after the transition.

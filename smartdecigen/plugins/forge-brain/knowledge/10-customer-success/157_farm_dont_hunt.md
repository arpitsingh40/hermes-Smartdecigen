# V157 · FARM DON'T HUNT — Guy Smith
Tier 3 · Customer Success · Tree Memory

## ROOT
The most efficient growth lever in B2B SaaS is not new logo acquisition — it's farming existing customer relationships for expansion revenue. Most companies treat sales as "hunting" (acquiring new logos) while neglecting "farming" (growing existing accounts through upsell, cross-sell, and retention). Smith's framework: the farm is where 70-80% of lifetime value resides. Nurturing existing accounts costs 5-7x less than acquiring new ones. The metrics that matter: NRR (net revenue retention), expansion rate, logo retention, revenue retention, customer health score, time-to-expand.

## TRUNK
The farming framework: (1) Land properly — a bad-fit customer churns regardless of farming effort. Farm starts at sale. (2) Cultivate — every customer interaction should demonstrate value and identify expansion opportunities. (3) Harvest — time the expansion ask to the moment of highest value recognition (post-onboarding success, post-QBR, post-ROI report). (4) Re-sow — a customer who expanded once is likely to expand again. The farming team: CSMs (responsible for health and adoption), Account Managers (responsible for retention and expansion), and Account Executives (responsible for cross-sell into adjacent departments). Key metric: NRR. Best-in-class is 120%+.

## FRUIT
- WHEN growth is flat despite new logo acquisition → APPLY the farm audit: is NRR above 100%? If not, farming is underinvested.
- WHEN expansion is slow → APPLY harvest timing: are you asking for expansion at the moment of highest value recognition?

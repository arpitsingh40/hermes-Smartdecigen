# V141 · BUILT TO LAST — Jim Collins & Jerry Porras
Tier 3 · Strategy · Tree Memory

## ROOT
Visionary companies — those that have been industry leaders for 50+ years — share specific habits: they preserve a core ideology (purpose + values) while stimulating progress in everything else. They have Big Hairy Audacious Goals (BHAGs), cult-like cultures, and a relentless focus on building the company, not just the product. Great companies are built to last because they're more than any single product or leader.

## TRUNK
Key findings from 18 visionary companies: (1) Clock building, not time telling — build an organization that can thrive beyond any single leader or product. (2) "Genius of the AND" — pursue both purpose AND profit, continuity AND change. (3) Core ideology: enduring values + purpose that don't change. (4) BHAGs: 10-30 year audacious goals. (5) Cult-like cultures: strong, distinctive culture that people self-select into. (6) Try a lot and keep what works — evolution, not revolution. (7) Home-grown management — promote from within.

## FRUIT
- WHEN defining company identity → APPLY core ideology: what values would you hold even if they became a competitive DISADVANTAGE?
- WHEN setting long-term goals → APPLY BHAG: what's the 10-30 year goal that seems impossible but not insane?

# V151 · DISCIPLINED ENTREPRENEURSHIP — Bill Aulet
Tier 3 · Strategy · Tree Memory

## ROOT
Entrepreneurship is not a mysterious art — it's a discipline that can be learned. Aulet's 24-step framework (from MIT's entrepreneurship program) provides a step-by-step process for building an innovation-driven enterprise. The core sequence: market segmentation → beachhead market selection → end-user profile → TAM calculation → persona definition → full life cycle use case → high-level product specification → quantify value proposition → identify next 10 customers → define core → competitive position → customer decision-making unit → map the sales process → unit economics. The framework is linear in structure but iterative in practice.

## TRUNK
6 themes across 24 steps: (1) Who is your customer? — segmentation, beachhead, persona. (2) What can you do for them? — use case, value prop, core positioning. (3) How does your customer acquire your product? — decision-making unit, sales process. (4) How do you make money? — business model, pricing, LTV/CAC. (5) How do you design and build? — product plan, MVP. (6) How do you scale? — financial model, growth plan. The most critical step: beachhead market selection. Choose ONE market segment where you can dominate, prove your model, then expand. The #1 mistake: trying to serve everyone from day one.

## FRUIT
- WHEN entering a new market → APPLY beachhead selection: which one segment will you dominate first?
- WHEN defining the ideal customer → APPLY persona definition: who is the specific end user, what's their priority, what are their obstacles?

## SEEDS

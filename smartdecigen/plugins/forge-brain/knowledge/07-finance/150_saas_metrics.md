# V150 · SAAS METRICS 2.0 — David Skok (for Matrix Partners)
Tier 3 · Finance · Tree Memory

## ROOT
Skok's SaaS metrics framework is the industry standard for measuring subscription business health. The key insight: SaaS is a "leaky bucket" where every month some customers churn. Growth only happens when new customers > lost customers. Core metrics: MRR/ARR, churn rate, LTV, CAC, CAC payback, renewal rate, expansion rate, ARPU. The "golden metrics" of SaaS: (1) Negative churn — expansion revenue exceeds churned revenue, so the business grows even without new customers. (2) Magic number — SaaS sales efficiency. (3) CAC payback under 12 months. (4) LTV/CAC > 3.

## TRUNK
Key metrics: (1) ARR / MRR — the one-line summary of subscription revenue. (2) Churn rate: logo churn (% of customers lost) vs revenue churn (% of revenue lost). Revenue churn > logo churn = losing your biggest customers. (3) Net Revenue Retention (NRR): starting revenue + expansion - churn - downgrades / starting revenue. Above 100% is excellent. (4) CAC payback: total sales + marketing spend to acquire a customer / monthly gross profit per customer. Under 12 months is healthy. (5) LTV/CAC: lifetime value / cost to acquire. 3:1 is minimum; 5:1 is excellent. (6) Magic number: (current quarter ARR - prior quarter ARR) × 4 / prior quarter sales + marketing spend. Magic number > 1 means efficient growth. (7) Quick ratio: (new MRR + expansion MRR) / (churned MRR + contraction MRR). Above 4 means excellent growth efficiency.

## FRUIT
- WHEN reviewing SaaS metrics → APPLY the golden metrics: is NRR above 100%? Is CAC payback under 12 months? Is LTV/CAC above 3?
- WHEN growth feels expensive → APPLY magic number: is your sales/marketing spend generating efficient growth? Under 0.5 = inefficient.

## SEEDS
- "NRR above 100% means your existing customers grow you, even if you stop acquiring."
- "CAC payback under 12 months is healthy. Under 6 is excellent. Over 24 is a warning."
- "Magic number > 1 means you should invest in sales. Under 0.5 means fix efficiency first."

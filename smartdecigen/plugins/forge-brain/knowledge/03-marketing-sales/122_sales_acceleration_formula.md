# V122 · THE SALES ACCELERATION FORMULA — Mark Roberge
Tier 3 · Sales · Tree Memory

## ROOT
The single biggest scaling bottleneck in SaaS is the sales team. Roberge (HubSpot CRO, grew revenue from $0 to $100M) codifies a data-driven, process-oriented approach to building a sales team. The formula: hire the same successful profile every time, provide the same training, provide the same quantity/quality of leads, and hold to the same compensation plan — then use data to identify the bottleneck in the formula.

## TRUNK
Four components: (1) Hire: define your ideal sales profile using data, not gut — track attributes of top performers and hire to that profile. (2) Train: continuous, quantitative, buyer-centric — not one-off onboarding. (3) Manage: coaching cadence built on metrics — pipeline generation rate, win rate, average deal size, sales cycle length. (4) Compensate: design incentives that reward the behavior you want — not just revenue closed but pipeline built, retention, team contribution. Data reveals the bottleneck: if win rate is the problem, fix training/qualification, not hiring.

## BRANCHES
- Hiring: build a quantitative ideal rep profile from your top quartile's traits. Coachability, curiosity, prior success, intelligence. Hire against the profile every time.
- Training: buyer journey-based, not product-feature-based. Reps learn to sell the way customers buy, not recite feature lists.
- Demand generation: SDRs should be measured on pipeline generated, not meetings booked. Quality > quantity.
- Compensation: commission cap kills high-performance culture. On-target earnings should be achievable by 60-70% of reps, not 20%.
- Metrics-driven coaching: pipeline generation rate × win rate × average deal size = individual forecast. Coach the weakest component per rep.

## FRUIT
- WHEN hiring sales → APPLY ideal rep profile: define the traits of your top 3 reps, hire to that profile.
- WHEN sales are inconsistent → APPLY the formula: which of the 4 components (hire/train/manage/compensate) is the bottleneck?
- WHEN designing compensation → APPLY the behavior test: does this plan reward the behaviors that drive long-term value or short-term bookings?
- WHEN a rep is underperforming → APPLY metric decomposition: is the problem pipeline generation, win rate, deal size, or sales cycle? Coach the specific component.

## SEEDS
- "Build your ideal sales rep profile from data, not gut feel."
- "Train reps to sell the way customers buy, not recite feature lists."
- "If 80% of reps miss quota, the target is the problem, not the reps."
- "Caps on commission cap your growth."
- "Pipeline generation rate × win rate × deal size = your rep's math. Coach the weakest number."

# V33 · TO SELL IS HUMAN + THE PSYCHOLOGY OF SELLING — Daniel Pink & Brian Tracy
Tier 2 · Sales Mindset · Engine: Modern Selling · Tree Memory

## ROOT — the deepest truth
Selling is not a specialized activity for extroverts with fast-talking scripts — it is a universal human act of PERSUASION that everyone performs every day (pitching an idea, convincing a team, getting buy-in), and the modern buyer has more information than the seller, which inverts the old power dynamic: ABP (Always Be Connecting) replaces ABC (Always Be Closing).

## TRUNK — core thesis
Two canon works fused: Pink's TO SELL IS HUMAN (2012) reframes selling as 'moving people' — attunement (perspective-taking over assertiveness), buoyancy (the right ratio of positivity to negativity, 3:1), clarity (identifying the problem the buyer didn't articulate). Tracy's THE PSYCHOLOGY OF SELLING (2006) provides the inner game — the mental laws of success (self-concept, goal-setting, the 10x rule for prospecting) and the practical structure (the 7-step sales process from prospecting to follow-through). Together they form a complete system: Pink provides the WHY and the WHAT of modern selling (empathy over aggression, questions over statements), Tracy provides the HOW — the daily discipline of pipeline management, rejection-handling, and closing mechanics. The alpha-information age (buyers research before talking) means the seller's role is DIAGNOSIS, not DESCRIPTION: the seller adds value by uncovering a problem the buyer's Google search couldn't find.

## BRANCHES & LEAVES

### Branch 1 — Attunement: power of perspective-taking
├─ Pink: the best salespeople listen 2x more than they talk (proven in call-center data) — not assertiveness but accurate perspective-taking
├─ Attunement ≠ agreement; it's the ability to hear their frame, then find where your offering fits their reality
├─ Tracy: the 80/20 listening rule — 80% listening, 20% talking
└─ LEAF: call-recording audit: count talk/listen ratio per call; anything above 50/10/60 talk is lost deals due to undiagnosed need

### Branch 2 — Buoyancy: the 3:1 positivity ratio
├─ Pink: the ideal ratio of positive to negative messages in a pitch is 3:1 (Losada ratio from high-performing teams)
├─ Interrogative self-talk beats declarative: 'can I close this?' outperforms 'I will close this' — questions trigger intrinsic motivation
├─ Tracy: rejection is a numbers game — 'the 10x rule' (call 10x more to handle the 9 no's that precede the 1 yes)
└─ LEAF: after a rejection, the founder's self-talk should switch from 'I'm not good at this' to 'how many no's before the yes?' — reframing as probability flips the emotional impact

### Branch 3 — Clarity: the diagnostic pivot
├─ Pink: the new value of the seller is problem-finding, not problem-solving — the buyer can find features online, they pay for a diagnosis they didn't make themselves
├─ Clarity questions: 'what does your current solution cost you?' 'what would happen if you did nothing?'
├─ Tracy: benefit selling over feature selling — features are facts, benefits are what the facts MEAN to the buyer
└─ LEAF: the opening of every sales conversation should be a diagnostic question that delivers value regardless of purchase — if they learn something from the call itself, you've won before you pitch

### Branch 4 — The psychology of the salesperson (Tracy)
├─ Self-concept determines success — the seller who sees themselves as a helper, not a manipulator, projects congruent confidence
├─ Goal-setting: clarity of target drives performance — 'I will close ₹X this month' vs 'I hope to do well'
├─ The rejection resistance: Tracy says every 'no' is one step closer to a 'yes' if the pipeline is full enough
└─ LEAF: the founder's own sales psychology is the #1 bottleneck in early revenue — invest in the inner game before the outer tools

### Branch 5 — The new rules: social selling and non-sales selling
├─ Pink: non-sales selling — persuading internal teams, raising funds, recruiting — is a bigger part of the economy than direct sales
├─ Social selling: buyers find you before you find them — personal brand as lead generation
├─ Tracy: prospecting is the lifeblood — 'when you stop prospecting, your business starts dying'
└─ LEAF: founder time must be split between direct prospecting (Tracy: pipeline-first) and non-sales persuasion (Pink: moving internal/external stakeholders)

## FRUIT — how SmartDecigen applies this
- WHEN a founder hates 'selling' → APPLY reframe: they're already selling ideas to investors, hires, partners — Pink's non-sales selling gives them permission to own the skill
- WHEN sales calls are monologues → APPLY attunement fix: enforce the 80/20 talk rule; the call opens with a diagnostic question, not a pitch
- WHEN pipeline is thin → APPLY Tracy's prospecting discipline: set a daily prospect-count target, hold the founder to it for 30 days
- WHEN a founder is crushed by rejection → APPLY self-talk shift: replace 'I failed' with 'that was #7 — the yes is around #20'
- WHEN the product is commoditized → APPLY clarity pivot: the value is in the diagnosis, not the features — train the team to uncover problems Google can't answer

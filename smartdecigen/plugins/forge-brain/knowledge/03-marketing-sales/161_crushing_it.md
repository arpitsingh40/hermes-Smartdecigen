# V161 · CRUSHING IT! — Gary Vaynerchuk
Tier 3 · Brand · Tree Memory

## ROOT
Your personal brand is the most valuable business asset you control. Vaynerchuk's thesis: every entrepreneur should build a personal brand through content on social media — not polished corporate content, but authentic, daily documentation of the journey. The platforms change (Instagram, TikTok, LinkedIn, podcast, YouTube), but the principle is constant: give value first, sell second. Jab, jab, jab, right hook — provide value 3-4 times before asking for anything.

## TRUNK
Platform strategy: (1) Document, don't create — point a camera at what you're already doing instead of producing polished content. Authenticity beats production value. (2) Platform-native content: what works on LinkedIn (text posts) doesn't work on TikTok (vertical short-form video). Create for each platform's audience and algorithm. (3) Engagement: reply to every comment. Build community, not audience. (4) Consistency: daily posting for years. The algorithm rewards consistency more than quality. (5) Monetization: only after building attention. The monetization paths: speaking, books, products, services, investments.

## FRUIT
- WHEN building a founder brand → APPLY document-don't-create: point the camera at your work and share it daily.
- WHEN content isn't growing → APPLY jab-jab-hook: are you providing value 80% of the time and asking 20%?

# V29 · CONTAGIOUS — Jonah Berger
Tier 2 · Consumer Psychology · Engine: Word of Mouth · Tree Memory

## ROOT — the deepest truth
Word of mouth is the primary driver of purchase — more than advertising, more than search — yet nearly all marketing is built to be SEEN, not SHARED; content becomes contagious when it serves the carrier's social currency, not the brand's vanity.

## TRUNK — core thesis
Six principles (STEPPS) make ideas and products catch on: SOCIAL CURRENCY (people share what makes them look good — insider knowledge, remarkable finds); TRIGGERS (top-of-mind = tip-of-tongue — link your product to common environment cues); EMOTION (high-arousal emotions — awe, anger, amusement — drive sharing; low-arousal — contentment, sadness — suppress it); PUBLIC (visible signals that imitate — design products and behaviors to be seen); PRACTICAL VALUE (useful news travels — package your utility like a bargain alert); STORIES (information travels under the guise of narrative — build a trojan horse story people want to retell). Word of mouth is 2x more effective than paid media and 10x more effective at driving purchase.

## BRANCHES & LEAVES

### Branch 1 — Social Currency: share to look good
├─ People share things that signal expertise, status, or insider-ness — the remarkability of the product is secondary to how sharing it reflects on the sharer
├─ Create: scarcity (FOMO), exclusivity (invitation-only feels valuable), insider knowledge ('did you know?')
└─ LEAF: test every message: "would I share this to look smart / in-the-know / helpful?" — if not, it's not contagious

### Branch 2 — Triggers: frequency beats intensity
├─ The more often the environment reminds people of your product, the more they talk about it — a weak trigger in high rotation beats a strong trigger seen once
├─ Create a link: associate with a frequent behavior ('Mars bar before a run' vs 'Valentine's Day chocolate')
└─ LEAF: identify the daily habit customers already have; if your product can be its trigger, word-of-mouth compounds

### Branch 3 — Emotion: arousal drives action
├─ High-arousal positive (awe, excitement, amusement) and negative (anger, anxiety) both boost sharing; low-arousal (sadness, contentment) suppresses it
├─ Physiological arousal activates sharing behavior — design for feeling, not information density
└─ LEAF: when the message bores you writing it, it bores them sharing it — inject the arousal vector before distribution

### Branch 4 — Public: observable = imitable
├─ Products and behaviors that are visible get copied — 'Monkey see, monkey do' is the most powerful distribution channel
├─ Make the private public: yellow Livestrong bracelet made 'I support the cause' visible; pink razors made shaving a signal
└─ LEAF: audit the customer journey for invisibility — every touchpoint that could be public but isn't is lost free advertising

### Branch 5 — Practical Value: useful news travels
├─ People share what helps others; discounts and tips spread because they reduce the receiver's effort or cost
├─ The 'rule of 100': percentages feel bigger for <100 (<₹100 → % off > ₹ off), absolute numbers for >100
└─ LEAF: fold useful packaging around even commodity offers — the reason-to-share should be separate from the product itself

### Branch 6 — Stories: information under disguise
├─ A story carries the brand as a passenger, not the driver; the story must be so retellable that the brand tagalong is unavoidable
├─ Build a trojan horse: the brand is integral to the plot, not a sponsorship insert
└─ LEAF: collect the founder's real customer-transformation stories and strip until the brand is inseparable from the narrative arc

## FRUIT — how SmartDecigen applies this
- WHEN a product isn't being talked about → APPLY STEPPS audit: which principle is missing? (typically: no trigger in daily life, or no social currency in sharing)
- WHEN shareability is a metric → APPLY trigger mapping: find the frequent habit that can cue the product, build the association
- WHEN designing a referral program → APPLY social currency + practical value: what insider status does the referrer earn, what utility does the referee get
- WHEN a campaign feels ignorable → APPLY arousal check: is the emotion high-arousal? Amusement, awe, or outrage beat contentment
- WHEN a customer story isn't working → APPLY trojan horse test: is the brand essential to the plot, or could it be swapped?

# V23 · BUILDING A STORYBRAND — Donald Miller
Tier 1 · Brand Strategy · Engine: Messaging · Tree Memory

## ROOT — the deepest truth
Customers don't buy the best products; they buy the products they can understand FASTEST — and the brain understands through story, where the CUSTOMER is the hero and the brand is only the guide: if you position yourself as the hero, you become the customer's competitor.

## TRUNK — core thesis
The brain conserves calories: confusing messages get filtered out before evaluation ("if you confuse, you lose"). Story is the brain's native compression format. The SB7 framework: a CHARACTER (customer, who wants something) has a PROBLEM (external, internal, philosophical — companies sell solutions to external problems, but people BUY solutions to internal frustrations), meets a GUIDE (the brand, showing empathy + authority), who gives them a PLAN (3-step process that removes fog), CALLS them to action (direct CTA + transitional CTA), helping them avoid FAILURE (stakes — no stakes, no story) and achieve SUCCESS (paint the transformation). Every ambiguity in messaging is paid for in silence: the customer simply moves on.

## BRANCHES & LEAVES

### Branch 1 — The customer is the hero
├─ Brands that cast themselves as hero (our awards, our journey, our passion) compete with the customer's ego and lose
├─ The guide has two traits ONLY: empathy ("we understand how it feels") and authority (proof: numbers, testimonials, logos) — more self-story than that becomes hero-theft
└─ LEAF: audit the founder's homepage/pitch: count 'we/our' vs 'you/your' — the ratio predicts the leak

### Branch 2 — The three levels of problem
├─ External: the functional issue (I need snacks for my kid's tiffin)
├─ Internal: the frustration/doubt (I feel like a bad parent giving junk) — THIS drives purchase
├─ Philosophical: the ought (kids deserve honest food)
├─ Winning messaging names all three and sells the resolution of the INTERNAL one
└─ LEAF: ask the founder: what does your customer FEEL the moment before they search for you? That feeling is the real product

### Branch 3 — The grunt test & the one-liner
├─ Grunt test: a stranger reads your homepage 5 seconds and must answer: What do you offer? How does it make my life better? How do I buy?
├─ One-liner formula: Problem → Solution → Result ("Most parents worry snacks are junk. GreenLeaf makes grandmother-recipe snacks with clean labels, so kids eat happy and parents stay proud.")
└─ LEAF: make the founder pass the grunt test before spending a rupee on traffic — traffic to confusion is donation

### Branch 4 — The plan and the calls to action
├─ The plan: 3 steps that dissolve the fog of commitment ("Order a sampler → Pick favorites → Subscribe") — reduces perceived risk
├─ Direct CTA: buy now / book a call — must be visually loud and repeated (people don't act unless challenged to)
├─ Transitional CTA: free value (guide, sample, quiz) for the not-yet-ready — earns permission and reciprocity
└─ LEAF: every page and pitch needs BOTH CTAs; a polite single 'learn more' is a whisper in a storm

### Branch 5 — Stakes: failure and success
├─ No stakes, no story: name what the customer loses by not acting (tastefully — a pinch of salt, not terror)
├─ Success must be CONCRETE: paint the after-state (the kitchen shelf, the school tiffin, the compliment)
└─ LEAF: founders under-sell failure-avoidance; loss framing (Cialdini/Kahneman) makes stakes the cheapest persuasion upgrade

### Branch 6 — Repetition and the sound bite
├─ The one-liner goes everywhere: email signatures, packaging, team's answer at weddings — repetition builds the memory structure
└─ LEAF: if the founder's own team gives different answers to 'what do you do?', the brand has no message yet

## FRUIT — how SmartDecigen applies this
- WHEN a founder's pitch/site confuses → APPLY grunt test: force the three answers into one screen; clarity before creativity.
- WHEN messaging lists features → APPLY problem laddering: external → internal → philosophical; rewrite to sell the internal resolution.
- WHEN the brand talks about itself → APPLY hero-transfer: recast customer as hero, brand as guide with empathy + authority proof.
- WHEN conversion is weak → APPLY plan + dual CTA: add the 3-step plan, one loud direct CTA, one transitional CTA.
- WHEN writing any campaign → APPLY stakes check: what failure is avoided, what success is concretely pictured?

## SEEDS — injectable one-liners
- "If you confuse, you lose — clarity beats cleverness every time."
- "Your customer is the hero. You're the guide. Guides don't hog the spotlight."

- "No stakes, no story. What happens if they don't act?"

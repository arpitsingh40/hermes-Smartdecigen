# V26 · INFLUENCE: THE PSYCHOLOGY OF PERSUASION — Robert Cialdini
Tier 2 · Consumer Psychology · Engine: Consumer Psychology · Tree Memory

## ROOT — the deepest truth
Human compliance runs on automatic triggers — click, whirr — seven deep principles (reciprocity, commitment/consistency, social proof, liking, authority, scarcity, unity) that fire below reasoning; ethical marketers ALIGN offers with these levers, and wise founders learn to recognize when the levers are being pulled on THEM.

## TRUNK — core thesis
Evolution equipped us with compliance shortcuts because they're usually right. Each principle has a mechanism, an amplifier, and a defense: RECIPROCITY (favors oblige; concessions trigger counter-concessions — rejection-then-retreat), COMMITMENT/CONSISTENCY (small, public, effortful, voluntary commitments reshape self-image and escalate), SOCIAL PROOF (we copy others when uncertain — amplified by similarity and multitude), LIKING (similarity, compliments, cooperation, association), AUTHORITY (titles, trappings, credentials — and the trust-multiplier of arguing against your own interest), SCARCITY (loss framing beats gain framing; newly scarce > always scarce; competition for scarce resources inflames desire), UNITY (shared identity: 'one of us' beats 'like us').

## BRANCHES & LEAVES

### Branch 1 — Reciprocity
├─ The rule obliges repayment even for unrequested favors; small gift → disproportionate return
├─ Rejection-then-retreat: large ask refused → smaller ask (the real one) accepted — the concession FEELS like a favor
├─ Application: free samples, genuinely useful free tools/content (the transitional CTA), generous service recovery
└─ LEAF: the gift must land FIRST and feel personal + unexpected; reciprocity built into onboarding beats discounts

### Branch 2 — Commitment & consistency
├─ Small first steps (quiz, wishlist, trial, public review) create identity pressure to stay consistent
├─ Written > spoken; public > private; effortful > easy; voluntary > coerced
├─ Foot-in-the-door: tiny yes → larger yes; lowball works but burns trust (avoid)
└─ LEAF: design ladders of micro-commitments toward purchase; ask customers to articulate WHY they chose you (their own words bind)

### Branch 3 — Social proof
├─ Fires under UNCERTAINTY; amplified by SIMILARITY (people like me) and numbers
├─ Negative social proof backfires: 'many people litter' teaches littering — never advertise the prevalence of the bad behavior ('most carts get abandoned')
└─ LEAF: testimonials must match the prospect's segment (a Kochi mother for Kochi mothers); show counts only when impressive and specific

### Branch 4 — Liking & unity
├─ Similarity, honest compliments, cooperative framing ('we're solving this together'), attractive association
├─ Unity is deeper: family, place, shared struggle — 'from our kitchen in Kochi, for Kerala tiffins'
└─ LEAF: founder-story marketing works through unity + liking; local identity is a small brand's unfair advantage

### Branch 5 — Authority
├─ Credentials, uniforms, expert endorsement — but the strongest: TRUSTWORTHY authority = admit a weakness before the strength ('it's not the cheapest — here's why')
└─ LEAF: place proof (certifications, lab tests, press) at decision points; script the honest drawback into the pitch

### Branch 6 — Scarcity
├─ Loss language > gain language (Kahneman's loss aversion in sales clothes); deadlines and limited runs mobilize
├─ Newly scarce is most potent; manufactured fake scarcity destroys trust when discovered
└─ LEAF: use TRUE scarcity only (small-batch reality, seasonal); frame as what they'll MISS, not what they'll get

## FRUIT — how SmartDecigen applies this
- WHEN designing funnels/offers → APPLY principle mapping: which of the 7 levers does each step legitimately use? Add the missing cheap ones (reciprocity gift, micro-commitment, segment-matched proof).
- WHEN conversion stalls despite traffic → APPLY uncertainty diagnosis: uncertainty is social proof's trigger — add similar-other testimonials and authority marks at the decision point.
- WHEN the founder faces a persuasive counterparty (distributor, investor, vendor) → APPLY defense mode: name which levers are being pulled (deadline = scarcity; free favor = reciprocity debt; 'everyone signed' = social proof) and separate the lever from the merits.
- WHEN writing offers → APPLY loss framing: what does the customer lose by waiting — stated truthfully.
- WHEN building community → APPLY unity: shared identity language beats feature language.

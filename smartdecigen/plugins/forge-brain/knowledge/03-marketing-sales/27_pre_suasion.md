# V27 · PRE-SUASION — Robert Cialdini
Tier 2 · Consumer Psychology · Engine: Consumer Psychology · Tree Memory

## ROOT — the deepest truth
The biggest lever of persuasion is the MOMENT BEFORE the message: whatever holds attention right then dominates judgment ("what's focal seems causal and important"), so the master persuader wins by choosing what the audience is thinking about immediately prior — the privileged moment.

## TRUNK — core thesis
Pre-suasion = arranging attention so the audience is sympathetic BEFORE the content arrives. Attention is zero-sum and channeled: openers, environments, questions, and images prime specific associations, and the primed concept temporarily rules evaluation. Asking "are you adventurous?" before a pitch multiplies trial sign-ups; a comfort-image background raises comfort-weighted choices. Key machinery: focal = causal (whatever attention rests on gets credited), unfinished tasks magnetize (Zeigarnik — open loops hold attention), if/when-then plans automate follow-through, and UNITY (shared 'we') is the pre-suasive relationship frame that makes all other levers stronger.

## BRANCHES & LEAVES

### Branch 1 — Privileged moments
├─ Judgment is disproportionately shaped by what's in-mind at decision time; create the moment, then ask
├─ The single-question opener that channels identity ('do you consider yourself someone who invests in health?') — answered yes, the pitch rides the self-image
└─ LEAF: design every pitch/page/call with a deliberate OPENER whose only job is to summon the right frame before the offer

### Branch 2 — Focal = causal = important
├─ What attention lands on gets weight and blame/credit: the first image on the site, the first line of the ad, the first topic in the meeting
├─ Backgrounds, metaphors and environments count: fluffy clouds sell comfort, coins sell price focus
└─ LEAF: audit the founder's first-five-seconds surfaces: what concept does each one summon — and is that the concept the offer needs?

### Branch 3 — Openers that work (and their ethics)
├─ Questions channel: yes-framing questions build a commitment runway
├─ Mystery/open loops: unanswered questions hold attention through entire messages (start with the puzzle, resolve at the CTA)
├─ Warning: channeling toward frames irrelevant to true merits is manipulation and burns discovered trust
└─ LEAF: open loops in content ('the pricing mistake almost every founder makes — in a minute') keep reading; close them honestly

### Branch 4 — Geographies of influence
├─ Environment primes behavior: writing sales copy while looking at customer photos changes the copy; office cues change negotiations
├─ Self-priming: surround the decision moment with cues of the identity you want active (craftsmanship, thrift, boldness)
└─ LEAF: prescribe environment design for the founder's OWN big decisions — and for the customer's checkout moment

### Branch 5 — Zeigarnik & implementation intentions
├─ Unfinished tasks occupy the mind rent-free — the cliffhanger mechanic; completion releases attention
├─ If/when-then plans ('when the invoice clears, I will call the distributor') triple follow-through by pre-loading the trigger
└─ LEAF: convert every founder commitment into when-then form; convert marketing sequences into open-loop chains

### Branch 6 — Unity as pre-suasion
├─ 'We' relationships (kin, place, co-creation) change the LISTENING, not just the message; co-creation (asking advice) merges identities
├─ Asking customers for ADVICE (not opinion) makes them partners — advisors buy
└─ LEAF: replace 'feedback surveys' with advice requests; the wording flips the relationship

## FRUIT — how SmartDecigen applies this
- WHEN a pitch/campaign is being designed → APPLY opener engineering: choose the single concept that must be in-mind first; build the question/image/line that summons it.
- WHEN pages convert poorly despite good offers → APPLY focal audit: what do the first seconds make focal — price, risk, or the desired identity/benefit?
- WHEN a founder needs a customer/partner meeting to go well → APPLY privileged-moment prep: the first topic raised will color everything; sequence deliberately.
- WHEN follow-through fails (founder or customer) → APPLY when-then conversion: attach every intended action to a concrete trigger.
- WHEN deepening customer relationships → APPLY advice-asking: 'what would you do in our place?' converts audiences into allies.

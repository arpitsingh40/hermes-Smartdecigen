# V155 · CONTENT CHEMISTRY — Andy Crestodina
Tier 3 · Marketing · Tree Memory

## ROOT
Most content marketing fails because it's created without understanding content's place in the marketing funnel. Crestodina's framework: content must be designed for specific stages of the buyer journey (awareness → consideration → decision) AND for specific channels (search, social, email). The core insight: content strategy has two sides — content creation (what you make) and content promotion (how it gets found). Most companies obsess over creation and ignore promotion. The result: great content nobody sees.

## TRUNK
Content types by funnel stage: Awareness (blog posts, infographics, videos — educational, not promotional), Consideration (case studies, webinars, comparison guides — proving value), Decision (testimonials, free trials, ROI calculators — removing risk). Content promotion: SEO (keyword research, on-page optimization, link building), social media (shareability, platform-native content), email (newsletter, drip campaigns), paid promotion (boosting high-performing content). Analytics: measure content ROI — traffic, engagement, conversion rate per content piece. Double down on what works.

## FRUIT
- WHEN content isn't driving results → APPLY the creation/promotion split: are you spending 20% on creation and 80% on promotion, or the reverse?
- WHEN building content strategy → APPLY funnel mapping: what stage is each content piece for? Is every stage covered?

## SEEDS

- "A great blog post nobody sees is zero ROI. Spend 80% on promotion."
